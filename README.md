# Animal Crossing Revenue Optimization Analysis (Excel)

## Project Overview

In this project, I used Microsoft Excel to analyze in-game economic data from Animal Crossing and model different revenue strategies.

I focused on two income sources:

- Turnip price trading (weekly price fluctuations)
- Deep sea creature sales (consistent revenue stream)

The goal was to compare profitability, evaluate risk vs return, and calculate total weekly earnings.

---

## Tools & Skills Used

- Pivot Tables  
- VLOOKUP  
- SUMIF  
- LEFT / RIGHT / MID / FIND  
- Percentage and profit calculations  
- Multi-sheet aggregation  

---

## Turnip Trading Analysis

I built a model to calculate:

- Profit per turnip  
- Percentage return  
- Weekly total profit  

Profit per turnip = Sell Price – Buy Price  
Weekly profit = Profit × 1000 turnips  

### Key Insights

- Small price changes significantly impacted total profit.
- Selling too early could result in losses.
- High-spike weeks generated large returns.
- Trading income was more volatile than fishing revenue.

This mirrors real-world speculative trading and timing strategies.

---

## Deep Sea Creature Revenue Analysis

I categorized creatures by rarity and calculated total revenue contribution using VLOOKUP and SUMIF.

### Key Insights

- Ultra-rare creatures contributed a disproportionate share of revenue.
- Revenue was heavily concentrated in high-value categories.
- Increasing high-rarity catches significantly improved earnings.
